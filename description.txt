Terraria ChatGPT brings conversational help, live web search, and personal goal notes into Terraria.

Ask about recipes, equipment, bosses, or anything else. Read clear answers with colored highlights for important items, locations, supplies, and missing materials. Describe a goal and choose "Set goal?" to pin a checklist. Keep up to six notes, drag or collapse them, and follow supported progress automatically while playing.

Choose your ChatGPT model and reasoning effort directly at the top of GPT chat. Available options follow your connected account and selected model. Close chat while waiting: a silent, fading answer preview and unread * let you know when the reply arrives without interrupting movement or clicks.

GPT can remember stable preferences and item-sorting corrections from normal conversation. Saved memories follow each character across worlds and normal Save & Quit/restarts. A Memory saved notice offers Undo; the Memory panel lets you review, remove or pause entries. Temporary counts and progress are kept out of long-term memory. Saved preferences are shared with online answers while Memory is enabled, separately from observation sharing.

The mod runs inside tModLoader. No separate companion, Python, or Node.js is needed. Connect an eligible ChatGPT account in mod settings, or explicitly choose the separately billed OpenAI API. Internet and available account allowance are required for online answers. Account and search availability depend on the connected service. Windows is required for this release's encrypted credential storage.

Optional progress sharing uses your own items, remembered chests you opened or placed in single-player, recorded boss defeats, achievements, and observed biome/NPC progress. Ask about NPC happiness, price modifiers and housing changes using readings recorded while speaking to NPCs. Answers use the running Terraria version. Turn observation sharing off in settings if you prefer. Hidden locations and unopened storage are never scanned, and GPT cannot spawn, kill, teleport, or change your game. General advice can still be wrong.

Ask GPT to categorise inventory by your chest names, then use Review sort or Sort chests to see its exact choices. Remember choices saves the reviewed preferences for future sorting; Confirm sort is the separate action that moves items. Hotbar, favorites, equipment and dedicated coin/ammo slots stay protected. Unassigned items stay in your inventory. Previously opened or player-placed chests remain known after normal saves/restarts; transfers still check current chest identity, space and vanilla Quick Stack range. Ordinary single-player chests are supported. Local sorting works without AI; new GPT destination suggestions require observation sharing.
